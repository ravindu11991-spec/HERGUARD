04 / OUR APPROACH

Safety tools should empower, not alarm.

HERGUARD is a student hackathon prototype designed to make online safety easier to understand. It encourages people to pause, verify information through independent channels and choose the next step that feels safe for them.

01
Privacy-conscious

Message analysis runs in your browser; checklist progress is stored locally on your device.

02
Clear about limits

Heuristic checks can miss threats or flag legitimate messages. No tool can guarantee safety.

03
Practical and respectful

Information is educational. People facing abuse should choose steps appropriate to their circumstances.
